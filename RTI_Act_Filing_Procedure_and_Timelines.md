# Topic: Right to Information - Filing Application, Fee, and Disposal Timelines
# Source: RTI Act 2005 - Chapter II (Sections 6-7)

### Section 6: Request for obtaining information
(1) A person who desires to obtain information under this Act shall make a request in writing or through electronic means in English, Hindi, or the official language of the area, accompanying the prescribed fee, to:
  (a) the CPIO or SPIO of the concerned public authority; or
  (b) the CAPIO or SAPIO,
specifying the particulars of the information sought.
* *Proviso:* Where a request cannot be made in writing, the CPIO/SPIO shall render all reasonable assistance to the person making the oral request to reduce it to writing.

(2) An applicant requesting information **shall not be required to give any reason** for requesting the information or any personal details except those necessary for contact.

(3) Where an application is made to a public authority requesting information that is held by another public authority or closely connected to another public authority's functions, the application (or relevant part) shall be transferred to that other authority immediately, and the applicant informed.
* *Proviso:* Transfer of an application under this sub-section must be made as soon as practicable and **no later than 5 days** from the date of receipt.

---

### Section 7: Disposal of request
(1) On receipt of a request under Section 6, the CPIO/SPIO shall, as expeditiously as possible, and **within 30 days** of receipt, either provide the information on payment of the prescribed fee or reject the request for reasons specified under Sections 8 and 9.
* *Proviso:* Where the information sought concerns the **life or liberty of a person**, it shall be provided **within 48 hours** of receipt of the request.

(2) Failure by the CPIO/SPIO to give a decision within 30 days shall be deemed a refusal of the request.

(3) If further fees representing the cost of providing information are required, the CPIO/SPIO shall notify the applicant with fee calculation details and inform them of their right to review/appeal. The intervening period between sending the notice and payment of fees is excluded from calculating the 30-day period.

(4) CPIO/SPIO shall assist sensorily disabled applicants to enable access and inspection of records.

(5) Fees charged shall be reasonable, and **no fee shall be charged from individuals below the poverty line (BPL)**.

(6) If the public authority fails to comply with specified time limits, the information **shall be provided free of charge**.

(7) CPIO/SPIO shall consider third-party representations under Section 11 before deciding.

(8) Rejection notices must communicate: (i) reasons for rejection, (ii) the appeal period, and (iii) details of the Appellate Authority.
