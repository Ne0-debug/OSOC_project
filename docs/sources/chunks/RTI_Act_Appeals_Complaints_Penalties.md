# Topic: Right to Information - Complaints, Appeals, Powers, and Penalties
# Source: RTI Act 2005 - Chapter V (Sections 18-20)

## CHAPTER V: POWERS, APPEALS, AND PENALTIES

### Section 18: Powers and Functions of Information Commissions
(1) The Central or State Information Commission shall receive and inquire into complaints from any person who:
  (a) has been unable to submit an RTI request because no PIO/APIO was designated or accepted the application;
  (b) has been refused access to requested information;
  (c) has not received a response within prescribed time limits;
  (d) considers requested fees unreasonable;
  (e) believes they were given incomplete, misleading, or false information.

(2) Commissions may initiate inquiries if satisfied that reasonable grounds exist.

(3) While inquiring into matters under this section, Commissions hold the same powers as a civil court under the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908 regarding summoning witnesses, inspecting documents, receiving affidavit evidence, and requisitioning public records.

(4) No record under the control of a public authority may be withheld from the Commission during an inquiry.

---

### Section 19: Appeals Procedure
(1) **First Appeal:** Any person aggrieved by a PIO decision, or who does not receive a decision within the Section 7 timeframe, may appeal **within 30 days** to a senior officer in rank to the PIO within the public authority (First Appellate Authority).

(2) Third-party appeals against Section 11 disclosure orders must be made **within 30 days**.

(3) **Second Appeal:** A second appeal against a First Appellate Authority decision lies **within 90 days** with the Central or State Information Commission.

(4) In appeal proceedings, the burden of proving that a denial of a request was justified rests entirely on the PIO.

(5) First appeals shall be disposed of **within 30 days** (extendable to a maximum of **45 days** for written reasons).

(6) Decisions of the Information Commission are binding and can order information provision, PIO appointments, record management changes, training, or financial compensation to the complainant.

---

### Section 20: Penalties
(1) If a Commission finds during a complaint or appeal that a PIO without reasonable cause refused an application, delayed information, malafidely denied requests, gave false/incomplete data, or destroyed records, it shall impose a penalty of **₹250 per day** until the information is provided.
* *Maximum Penalty:* Total penalty amount shall not exceed **₹25,000**.
* *Proviso:* The PIO must be given a reasonable opportunity of being heard, with the burden of proof resting on the PIO.

(2) Persistent failure without reasonable cause shall result in recommendations for **disciplinary action** under applicable service rules.
