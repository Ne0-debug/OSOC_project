# Topic: Right to Information - Miscellaneous Provisions, Exempt Organizations, and Rule-Making
# Source: RTI Act 2005 - Chapter VI (Sections 21-30)

## CHAPTER VI: MISCELLANEOUS

### Section 21 & 22: Legal Protection and Overriding Effect
* **Section 21:** No suit or prosecution lies against any person for anything done in good faith under this Act or its rules.
* **Section 22:** The provisions of this Act **override** anything inconsistent contained in the Official Secrets Act, 1923, or any other existing law/instrument.

---

### Section 23: Bar of Jurisdiction of Courts
No court shall entertain any suit or proceeding in respect of any order made under this Act; orders can only be questioned through the appeal process specified under this Act.

---

### Section 24: Act Not to Apply to Certain Intelligence/Security Organisations
(1) This Act does not apply to intelligence and security organisations specified in the Second Schedule.
* *Exception 1:* Information pertaining to **allegations of corruption and human rights violations** is NOT exempt.
* *Exception 2:* Information requested regarding human rights violations requires Information Commission approval and shall be provided **within 45 days**.

---

### Section 25 & 26: Monitoring, Reporting, and Government Programmes
(1) Information Commissions shall prepare an annual implementation report forwarded to the appropriate Government and laid before Parliament/State Legislature.

(2) Governments shall develop educational programmes, train PIOs, and compile easily comprehensible guidelines and user manuals within 18 months of enactment.

---

### Section 27, 28, & 29: Power to Make Rules
Appropriate Governments and competent authorities hold the power to issue notifications specifying fee structures, commission procedures, and service terms, all of which must be laid before Parliament or the State Legislature for 30 days.
