# Topic: Right to Information - Exemptions from Disclosure and Third Party Rules
# Source: RTI Act 2005 - Chapter II (Sections 8-11)

### Section 8: Exemption from disclosure of information
(1) There shall be no obligation to disclose to any citizen:
  (a) Information affecting the sovereignty, integrity, security, strategic, or economic interests of India, or leading to incitement of an offence;
  (b) Information expressly forbidden by a court or tribunal, or constituting contempt of court;
  (c) Information causing a breach of privilege of Parliament or State Legislature;
  (d) Commercial confidence, trade secrets, or intellectual property harming a third party's competitive position, unless larger public interest warrants disclosure;
  (e) Information held in a fiduciary relationship, unless larger public interest warrants disclosure;
  (f) Information received in confidence from a foreign government;
  (g) Information endangering life, physical safety, or identifying confidential law enforcement sources;
  (h) Information impeding investigation, apprehension, or prosecution of offenders;
  (i) Cabinet papers including Council of Ministers deliberations (decisions and supporting material shall be made public after completion/decision);
  (j) Personal information with no relationship to public activity or interest, or causing unwarranted invasion of privacy, unless larger public interest justifies it.
* *Proviso:* Information that cannot be denied to Parliament or a State Legislature shall not be denied to any person.

(2) Public interest in disclosure outweighs harm to protected interests notwithstanding the Official Secrets Act, 1923.

(3) Information relating to events occurring **20 years prior** to the request date shall be provided, subject to clauses (a), (c), and (i) of Section 8(1).

---

### Section 9: Grounds for rejection
Access may be rejected if it involves infringement of copyright subsisting in a person other than the State.

---

### Section 10: Severability
If a request is rejected because parts of the record are exempt, non-exempt parts that can reasonably be severed shall be provided to the applicant alongside written explanation notices.

---

### Section 11: Third party information
(1) If requested information relates to or was supplied by a third party and treated as confidential by them, the CPIO/SPIO shall, **within 5 days**, give written notice to the third party inviting written or oral submissions.

(2) The third party shall be given **10 days** from receipt of notice to make representation against disclosure.

(3) The CPIO/SPIO shall make a decision on disclosure **within 40 days** of receiving the Section 6 request and notify the third party.

(4) Third parties are entitled to prefer an appeal under Section 19 against disclosure decisions.
