# Topic: Right to Information - Duties and Obligations of Public Authorities
# Source: RTI Act 2005 - Chapter II (Sections 3-5)

## CHAPTER II: RIGHT TO INFORMATION AND OBLIGATIONS OF PUBLIC AUTHORITIES

### Section 3: Right to information
Subject to the provisions of this Act, all citizens shall have the right to information.

---

### Section 4: Obligations of public authorities
(1) Every public authority shall—
  (a) maintain all its records duly catalogued and indexed in a manner that facilitates the right to information under this Act and ensure that all records appropriate for computerisation are network-connected within reasonable time and resources;
  (b) publish within 120 days from the enactment of this Act details regarding its organization, functions, duties, officer powers, decision-making processes, manuals, remuneration, budgets, and Public Information Officers (PIOs), updating these publications annually;
  (c) publish all relevant facts while formulating important policies or announcing decisions affecting the public;
  (d) provide reasons for its administrative or quasi-judicial decisions to affected persons.

(2) It shall be a constant endeavour of every public authority to take steps to provide as much information *suo motu* (on its own initiative) to the public at regular intervals through various means, including the internet, minimizing the need for citizens to use this Act to obtain information.

(3) Information shall be disseminated widely and in an easily accessible form and manner.

(4) All materials shall be disseminated considering cost-effectiveness, local language, and effective communication methods in the area.

---

### Section 5: Designation of Public Information Officers (PIOs)
(1) Every public authority shall, within 100 days of enactment, designate Central Public Information Officers (CPIOs) or State Public Information Officers (SPIOs) in all administrative units to provide information to applicants.

(2) Every public authority shall designate an officer at each sub-divisional or sub-district level as a Central Assistant Public Information Officer (CAPIO) or State Assistant Public Information Officer (SAPIO) to receive applications or appeals and forward them immediately to the CPIO/SPIO or Appellate Authority.
* *Proviso:* Where an application or appeal is submitted to an Assistant PIO (CAPIO/SAPIO), a period of 5 days shall be added to the response timeline specified under Section 7(1).

(3) CPIOs/SPIOs shall deal with requests and render reasonable assistance to information seekers.

(4) A CPIO/SPIO may seek the assistance of any other officer for the proper discharge of their duties.

(5) Any officer whose assistance is sought under sub-section (4) shall render assistance and be treated as a CPIO/SPIO for the purpose of any contravention of this Act.
