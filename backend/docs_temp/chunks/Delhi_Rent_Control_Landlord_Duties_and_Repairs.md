# Delhi Rent Control Act, 1958 - Chapter IV: Repairs & Essential Services Maintenance

## Section 44: Maintenance of Essential Services
* **Prohibition**: No landlord shall, without just or sufficient cause, cut off or withhold essential supplies or services (water, electricity, passage, sanitary, elevator).
* **Emergency Orders**:
  * If cut off, the tenant may file an application before the Rent Controller.
  * Controller may issue an immediate interim directive ordering the landlord to restore services within **24 to 48 hours**.
  * Penalty for willful withholding: Fines or compensation awarded to tenant up to **₹500**.

## Section 45: Duty to Maintain and Repair Premises
* **Landlord Obligation**: Landlord must keep premises in good, tenantable condition.
* **30-Day Repair Notice**:
  * If landlord neglects repairs, tenant serves a formal **30-day written notice**.
  * If landlord fails to repair within 30 days, tenant may apply to Controller for permission to make repairs.
  * **Cost Deduction Limit**: Tenant can deduct actual repair costs from rent, up to a maximum limit of **1/12th (one month) of total annual rent** for that year.
