# Delhi Rent Control Act, 1958 - Chapter V & VI: Rent Deposit & Summary Proceedings

## Section 27: Deposit of Rent by Tenant
A tenant may deposit rent directly with the Rent Controller under the following circumstances:
1. Landlord refuses or neglects to accept rent tendered by the tenant.
2. Landlord refuses or fails to issue a written receipt for rent received.
3. Genuine dispute or doubt exists as to who is entitled to receive the rent.

### Procedure for Rent Deposit
* Application must be filed along with full rent amount within **21 days** of refusal/dispute.
* Rent Controller issues notice to landlord to accept deposit; protects tenant from default under Section 14(1)(a).

## Section 25B: Special Summary Procedure for Bona Fide Eviction
Special procedure applying exclusively to eviction applications filed under Section 14(1)(e) (Bona Fide Requirement):
* **Summons Issue**: Controller issues special summons requiring tenant appearance.
* **15-Day Leave to Defend**:
  * Tenant MUST file an affidavit seeking leave to contest within **15 days** of receiving summons.
  * If tenant fails to file within 15 days, statements in landlord's application are deemed admitted, and an eviction order is issued automatically.
* **No Appeal**: Orders passed under Section 25B cannot be appealed to the Rent Control Tribunal; only revision before the Delhi High Court is permitted.
