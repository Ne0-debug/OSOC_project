# Topic: Statutory refund timelines, interest penalties, and unauthorized retention of security deposit

# Model Tenancy Act, 2021 - Deposit Refund Timelines & Penalty Provisions

## Statutory Refund Timeline
* **Handover Date Requirement**: The landlord must return the entire security deposit (minus mutually agreed, itemized lawful deductions) on or before the exact date and time the tenant vacates the property and hands over keys/possession.
* **Itemized Statement Obligation**: If any deduction is claimed, the landlord MUST provide an itemized written statement detailing every expense, accompanied by original receipts, contractor invoices, or utility bills justifying the deduction.

## Unauthorized Retention & Penalty Interest
* If a landlord fails or refuses to refund the security deposit at the time of vacating without providing a valid, itemized justification:
  * The retention is categorized as **unlawful withholding of tenant property**.
  * The landlord becomes liable to pay simple interest on the delayed refund amount for the entire period of delay.
  * Interest rates are determined by the Rent Authority, typically aligned with standard commercial bank lending rates (or specified statutory penalty interest rates up to 15% to 18% per annum depending on local state rules).

## Unilateral Deductions & Tenant Protections
* A landlord cannot unilaterally estimate damage repair costs without independent verification or tenant consent.
* If a dispute arises over disputed damage claims:
  * The tenant can accept the undisputed portion of the deposit under protest.
  * The tenant can immediately file an application before the Rent Authority to recover the withheld amount along with penal interest and litigation costs.
