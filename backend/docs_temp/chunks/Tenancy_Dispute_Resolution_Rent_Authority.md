# Topic: Dispute resolution process, jurisdiction, and filing complaint before Rent Authority

# Model Tenancy Act, 2021 - Dispute Resolution Framework

## Exclusive Jurisdiction of Rent Authorities
* To ensure fast-track resolution of landlord-tenant conflicts, Section 33 of the Act explicitly bars Civil Courts from entertaining suits related to tenancy disputes, security deposit recovery, or evictions covered under this Act.
* All disputes must be adjudicated through a dedicated three-tier mechanism:
  1. **Rent Authority**: First-instance administrative authority headed by an officer not below the rank of Deputy Collector.
  2. **Rent Court**: Presided over by an Additional District Judge or District Judge.
  3. **Rent Tribunal**: Appellate body for higher legal challenges.

## Procedure to File a Security Deposit Recovery Application
If a landlord refuses to return the security deposit after vacating:
1. **Notice Stage**: The tenant issues a formal Legal Notice / Pre-litigation Demand Notice to the landlord giving 15 days to refund the amount.
2. **Application Submission**: If the landlord fails to respond or refund within 15 days, the tenant files an application under Section 31 before the local **Rent Authority** having territorial jurisdiction over the property location.
3. **Essential Information Required in Complaint**:
   * Full name, contact details, and current address of the Applicant (Tenant).
   * Full name and address of the Respondent (Landlord).
   * Property address, monthly rent amount, and original security deposit paid (with payment proof/bank receipt).
   * Date of tenancy termination and physical handover date.
   * Total withheld amount and breakdown of relief claimed (Principal refund + Interest + Costs).

## Powers and Orders of the Rent Authority
Upon receiving an application, the Rent Authority issues notice to the landlord, conducts summary proceedings within a target resolution timeframe of **60 days**, and can issue binding orders to:
* Direct the landlord to pay the full withheld deposit immediately.
* Award penal interest on delayed payments calculated from the date of handover.
* Award compensation and litigation costs to the tenant for harassment or illegal delay.
