# Delhi Rent Control Act, 1958 - Chapter I: Preliminary, Applicability & Definitions

## Section 1: Short Title, Extent, and Commencement
* **Extent**: Applies to the entire Union Territory of Delhi, specifically urban areas within the jurisdiction of:
  * Municipal Corporation of Delhi (MCD)
  * New Delhi Municipal Committee (NDMC)
  * Delhi Cantonment Board
* **Government Power**: The Central Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, extend or withdraw applicability to any other rural or urban areas.

## Section 2: Key Statutory Definitions
* **Tenant (Section 2(l))**: Any person by whom or on whose account rent is payable for any premises. Includes:
  * Any person continuing in possession after termination of tenancy (statutory tenant).
  * Sub-tenants who were lawfully inducted prior to commencement.
  * In case of a deceased residential tenant, the right extends to surviving spouse, son, daughter, or parents who were ordinarily residing with the tenant as family members at the time of death.
* **Landlord (Section 2(e))**: Any person receiving or entitled to receive rent, whether on own account, as an agent, trustee, guardian, or receiver.
* **Premises (Section 2(i))**: Any building or part of a building let for residence, business, trade, or commercial use, including:
  * Gardens, grounds, outhouses, garages, or storage structures attached to the building.
  * Fixtures and fittings provided by the landlord for beneficial enjoyment.
  * *Exclusions*: Does not include rooms in hotel, hostel, or lodging house.
* **Controller (Section 2(b))**: A Rent Controller or Additional Rent Controller appointed under Section 35 by the Central Government.
* **Standard Rent (Section 2(k))**: Rent fixed in accordance with Section 6 or basic rent plus permitted lawful increases.
