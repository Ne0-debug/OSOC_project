# Topic: Right to Information - Information Commissions Structure, Terms, and Removal
# Source: RTI Act 2005 - Chapters III & IV (Sections 13-17)

## CHAPTER III & IV: INFORMATION COMMISSIONS ADMINISTRATION

### Section 13 & 16: Terms of Office and Conditions of Service
(1) The Chief Information Commissioner (CIC) and State Chief Information Commissioner (SCIC) hold office for the term prescribed by the Central Government, up to a maximum age limit of 65 years, and are not eligible for reappointment.

(2) Information Commissioners (ICs) and State Information Commissioners (SICs) hold office for the prescribed term or until attaining 65 years of age, whichever is earlier. An IC/SIC is eligible for appointment as CIC/SCIC, provided the aggregate service does not exceed five years.

(3) Officers must make an oath or affirmation before the President or Governor before entering office.

(4) Salaries, allowances, and service conditions are prescribed by the Central Government and shall not be varied to the officer's disadvantage after appointment.

---

### Section 14 & 17: Removal of Information Commissioners
(1) The CIC, SCIC, IC, or SIC may be removed from office only by order of the President (or Governor for State Officers) on the ground of proved misbehaviour or incapacity, following an inquiry and report by the Supreme Court.

(2) The President or Governor may suspend an officer during the Supreme Court inquiry process.

(3) Notwithstanding sub-section (1), the President or Governor may directly order removal if the officer:
  (a) is adjudged an insolvent;
  (b) is convicted of an offence involving moral turpitude;
  (c) engages in paid employment outside the duties of the office during their term;
  (d) is unfit to continue in office due to infirmity of mind or body; or
  (e) acquires financial or other interests likely to prejudicially affect their official functions.

(4) Being interested in any contract or agreement made on behalf of the Government, or participating in profits thereof, constitutes proved misbehaviour.
