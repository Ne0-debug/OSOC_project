# Delhi Rent Control Act, 1958 - Chapter II: Rent Provisions & Unlawful Premium

## Section 4 & 5: Rent Restrictions & Prohibition of Premium
* **Lawful Rent Limit**: Landlords are prohibited from claiming or receiving rent exceeding the standard rent fixed under Section 6.
* **Prohibition of Pagree / Key Money (Section 5)**:
  * No person shall claim or receive any premium, sum, or financial consideration ("Pagree") in consideration of grant, renewal, or transfer of tenancy.
  * Advance rent deposit cannot exceed **one month's rent**.

## Section 6: Determination of Standard Rent
* **Residential Premises**: Calculated at **7.5% to 10% per annum** of the reasonable cost of construction plus market value of the land at the date of construction commencement.
* **Commercial Premises**: Calculated up to **10% per annum** of total capital investment value.

## Section 7 & 9: Lawful Rent Increases & Alterations
* **Structural Alterations (Section 7)**: Landlord carrying out lawful structural additions or improvements at their own expense may increase standard rent per annum by an amount not exceeding **10% of the expenditure incurred**.
* **Notice of Increase (Section 8)**: Any permitted rent increase requires a formal **30-day written notice** specifying the grounds and calculation.
* **Rent Revision Application (Section 9)**: Either party may apply to the Rent Controller for determination or adjustment of standard rent.
