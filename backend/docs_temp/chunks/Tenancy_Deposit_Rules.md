# Topic: Security deposit limits, statutory caps, and lawful deduction rules under tenancy law

# Model Tenancy Act, 2021 - Security Deposit Framework

## Statutory Limits on Security Deposits
Under Section 11 of the Model Tenancy Act, 2021, the maximum amount of security deposit that a landlord can demand in advance from a tenant is strictly capped based on the category of the property:
* **Residential Property**: Maximum of **two months' rent**.
* **Non-Residential / Commercial Property**: Maximum of **six months' rent**.

Any clause in a tenancy agreement or demand by a landlord exceeding these statutory caps is legally invalid and unenforceable before the Rent Authority.

## Refund Rules upon Possession Handover
* The landlord is under a statutory obligation to refund the security deposit (or the net balance after agreed lawful deductions) to the tenant on the same day the tenant delivers vacant physical possession of the premises back to the landlord.
* Payment must be processed via verifiable bank transfer, cheque, or electronic means.

## Scope of Lawful Deductions
Landlords cannot make arbitrary or blanket withholdings. Deductions from the security deposit are strictly limited to:
1. **Unpaid Arrears of Rent**: Outstanding rent amounts due for the period prior to vacating.
2. **Unpaid Utility Charges**: Unpaid electricity bills, water bills, gas charges, or municipal service charges incurred during the tenancy period.
3. **Intentional Structural Damage**: Direct physical damage caused intentionally or through negligence by the tenant, family members, or visitors.

## Express Deductions Exclusions
The following CANNOT be deducted from the tenant's security deposit:
* **Normal Wear and Tear**: Deterioration caused by reasonable usage, age, climate, or passage of time (e.g., paint fading, minor wall scuffs, routine plumbing wear).
* **Pre-existing Defects**: Repairs or damages that existed prior to the commencement of the tenancy.
* **Routine Maintenance**: Regular building maintenance responsibilities that belong to the landlord under Section 15 of the Act (e.g., major structural repairs, external painting, roof leakage).
