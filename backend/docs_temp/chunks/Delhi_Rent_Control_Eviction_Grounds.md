# Delhi Rent Control Act, 1958 - Chapter III: Protection Against Eviction

## Section 14: Protection of Tenant Against Eviction
No order or decree for eviction shall be passed by any court or Controller except on one or more specific statutory grounds:

### Statutory Eviction Grounds (Section 14(1))
* **Section 14(1)(a) - Default in Rent**:
  * Tenant fails to pay or tender arrears of rent within **two months** from the date of service of a formal notice of demand by registered post.
  * *First Default Benefit (Section 14(2))*: Eviction cannot be ordered if the tenant deposits the full arrears ordered by the Controller under Section 15(1).
* **Section 14(1)(b) - Sub-Letting Without Consent**:
  * Tenant sub-lets, assigns, or parts with possession of the whole or part of premises without obtaining prior **written consent** of the landlord.
* **Section 14(1)(c) - Misuse / Change of User**:
  * Tenant uses premises for a purpose other than that specified in the agreement, or causes public nuisance/damage to property despite notice to cease.
* **Section 14(1)(d) - Non-Residence**:
  * Residential premises left unused without reasonable cause by tenant or family for a continuous period of **six months** immediately preceding the application.
* **Section 14(1)(e) - Bona Fide Requirement**:
  * Landlord requires residential premises bona fide for occupation as residence for self or family dependent on them, having no other reasonably suitable accommodation.
* **Section 14(1)(h) - Alternative Accommodation**:
  * Tenant has built, acquired vacant possession of, or been allotted a suitable residential accommodation.
* **Section 14(1)(j) - Substantial Damage**:
  * Tenant has caused or permitted substantial damage to the premises affecting structural integrity.
