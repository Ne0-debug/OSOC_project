# Legal Sources & Statutory References: Tenancy Module

## Statutory Acts & Legal Authorities
1. **Model Tenancy Act, 2021**
   - **Enacting Authority**: Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA), Government of India.
   - **Key Provisions Referenced**:
     - *Section 11*: Statutory limits on security deposit (capped at 2 months' rent for residential, 6 months' rent for non-residential).
     - *Section 15*: Distinction between landlord structural maintenance responsibilities and regular tenant maintenance.
     - *Section 31 & 33*: Application procedure, 15-day pre-litigation notice, and exclusive jurisdiction of the Rent Authority, Rent Court, and Rent Tribunal (barring Civil Courts).
   - **Official Portal / Source**: [Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs - Model Tenancy Act](https://mohua.gov.in/)

2. **Standard Rent Control & Dispute Precedents**
   - **Pre-Litigation Demand Notice Requirements**: Civil procedure framework requiring a 15-day cure period notice before submitting deposit recovery applications to the local Rent Authority.
